## 📌 Project Overview

This project demonstrates an end-to-end **e-commerce sales analytics solution using Microsoft Power BI**.

The dashboard converts transactional sales and customer data into an interactive business intelligence report covering:

- Revenue and profit performance
- Order volume and units sold
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Product and category performance
- Regional and state-level sales
- Sales-channel performance
- Payment-method distribution
- Customer-level sales analysis
- Monthly sales and profit trends
- Quarter-based filtering

The project is designed as a portfolio-ready BI solution and follows a typical business reporting workflow: **data preparation → data modeling → KPI development → visualization → business insights**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard is intended to help business stakeholders answer questions such as:

1. How much revenue and profit did the business generate?

## 🏗️ Data Model

The Power BI file uses two primary business tables:

```text
CUSTOMER
   │
   │ CustomerID
   ▼
SALES
```

### CUSTOMER

Customer master/dimension data containing demographic, geographic, segment, and signup information.

### SALES

Transaction/fact data containing order-level sales, product, pricing, cost, profit, channel, payment, and geography information.

The model uses `CustomerID` as the key for connecting customer information to sales transactions.

## 🧮 Key Metrics

The project uses standard commercial KPIs.

### Total Sales

```text
Total Sales = SUM(SalesAmount)
```

### Total Profit

```text
Total Profit = SUM(Profit)
```

### Total Quantity

```text
Total Quantity = SUM(Quantity)
```

### Average Order Value

```text
AOV = Total Sales / Number of Orders
```

### Profit Margin

```text
Profit Margin = Total Profit / Total Sales
```

> KPI definitions should remain consistent across dashboards so that business users can compare reporting periods reliably.

### Category observations

- **Electronics** is the largest revenue category in the supplied data.
- **Accessories** has a comparatively high profit margin despite lower total revenue.
- **Furniture** and **Home Appliances** also show stronger margins than Electronics.
- Electronics generates substantial revenue but has a lower margin than several smaller categories.

### Product observations

The largest revenue contribution comes from high-value products such as:

- Laptop
- Smartphone
- Tablet
- Monitor

Lower-ticket products such as stationery items generate smaller revenue but show higher percentage margins in the supplied dataset.

### Channel observations

The supplied dataset contains three channels:

- Online
- Store
- Marketplace

Online represents the largest sales volume/value contribution in the dataset.

### Regional observations

The five regions are:

- West
- South
- North
- Central
- East

West has the largest sales contribution in the supplied transaction data, followed by South and North.

### Payment observations

UPI is the most frequently used payment method by transaction quantity in the supplied data, followed by Credit Card and Debit Card.

## ⚠️ Limitations

This project is primarily a **portfolio/demo analytics project**.

The dataset does not represent a verified production company's financial or customer data. Therefore:

- Findings are descriptive rather than predictive.
- No causal conclusions should be drawn from the dashboard alone.
- Customer behavior should not be generalized beyond this dataset.
- Production deployment would require governance, security, refresh automation, and validated business definitions.
- Public GitHub publication should use anonymized/non-sensitive data.

---

## 🔐 Data Privacy

Before publishing this project publicly, verify that the dataset does not contain:

- Personally identifiable information
- Real customer names
- Contact information
- Addresses
- Payment credentials
- Confidential business information

For a public portfolio repository, synthetic or anonymized data is recommended.
